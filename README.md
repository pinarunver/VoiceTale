# VoiceTale 🎙️

### AI-Powered Parent Voice Cloning & Personalized Storytelling Platform

---

## 📝 Overview
**VoiceTale** is an AI-driven mobile platform designed to clone parents' voices, allowing them to narrate personalized audiobooks for their children. The project aims to strengthen the emotional bond between children and parents through technology, providing a unique and personalized storytelling experience.

* **Academic Context:** Developed as a Senior Design Thesis for the Computer Engineering Department.
* ⚠️ **Note:** This repository serves as a **technical showcase and documentation** for the thesis project. It highlights the integration of zero-shot voice cloning and text-to-speech (TTS) pipelines.
* 👥 **Team:**  Pınar Ünver-Barış Aslan-Beytullah Tütüncü-Tuğçe Deniz

---

## 🚀 What It Does
VoiceTale automates the creation of personalized audio content by processing short parent voice samples through advanced AI pipelines. The platform delivers high-fidelity audiobooks, enabling children to listen to stories in their parents' authentic voices even when they are apart.

**Key capabilities:**
* 🎤 **Zero-Shot Voice Cloning** — Cloning a parent's voice with only a few seconds of audio input.
* 🎭 **Emotional Synthesis** — Utilizing XTTSv2 for natural, expressive, and emotionally resonant speech delivery.
* ☁️ **Cloud-Native Architecture** — Secure management of voice models and story libraries via Supabase and GitHub.
* 📱 **Mobile Integration** — A user-friendly interface designed for seamless voice recording and playback.
* 🔒 **Data Privacy** — Secure handling of personal voice data and user-specific storytelling libraries.
